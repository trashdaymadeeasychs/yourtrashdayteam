Why it matters

Why monthly bin washing matters in the Lowcountry

  • Kills the smell

    Charleston heat and humidity turn leftover residue into odor fast. Washing resets your cans every month.

  • Cuts pests

    Maggots, roaches, and flies feed on the film left in dirty bins. A clean can gives them nothing to live on.

  • Protects curb appeal

    Stained, grimy cans make a whole property look neglected. Clean bins look cared for.

  • Healthier handling

    You and your family or staff touch the lids and handles constantly. Cleaner cans mean less transfer.
